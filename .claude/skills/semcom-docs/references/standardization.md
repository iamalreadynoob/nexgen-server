# Standardization Status (as of October 2026)

SemCom is **not yet specified** in any 3GPP release. The activity is at the level of use cases, study reports and pre-standards.

When you write about standards, put the release or the date in the sentence. Example: "as of 3GPP Rel-20, Oct 2026".

| Body | Activity |
|---|---|
| **ITU-R WP 5D (IMT-2030)** | The framework and objectives for 6G (Rec. ITU-R M.2160, 2023) list *AI and communication* as a usage scenario. Submissions of candidate technologies are expected from **Feb 2027**. The final deadline is **Feb 2029**. |
| **IMT-2030 (6G) Promotion Group (China)** | It has a dedicated **SemCom task group**. It published technical reports on SemCom and on semantic-aware networking architecture. Industry and academic contributors focus on scenarios, KPIs, semantic HARQ, codebooks, generative SemCom, agent-to-agent communication and token communication. |
| **3GPP** | Release 20 is the 6G **study** phase (≈50% of the Rel-20 capacity). SA1 completed the 6G use cases and requirements (TR 22.870) in Q1 2026. SA1 accepted a SemCom-related use case on *AI-driven multi-vehicle cooperative perception*. **Release 21** will carry the first normative 6G specifications and the IMT-2030 submission. 3GPP treats AI/ML as a system-wide primitive (for example, two-sided CSI compression, which is conceptually close to learned JSCC). |
| **CCSA, ETSI, IEEE** | Research reports and special issues. ETSI ISGs and IEEE ComSoc activities on semantic and AI-native networking. |

## Gating factors for standardization

- Semantic KPIs that can be tested.
- Interoperable formats for features and KBs.
- Model lifecycle management (training, updating, versioning).
- Lightweight security primitives.
